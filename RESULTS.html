The full system is the only configuration that passes the gate on every seed. Parallel environments and value normalisation matter most for constraint satisfaction; per-agent advantages and rewards for cost; the Lagrangian dual closes the last seed; the attention critic accelerates convergence but is not necessary at full budget. no_shield is an integrity finding, not a cost result: €74 is below the perfect-foresight bound (€87.52), which is impossible — without the EV projection the policy learns to abandon EV charging. The shield is what makes the cost metric non-gameable. Full reading: docs/ABLATION.md.

6. Scope

Attention over an unordered household set, not a GNN (no feeder adjacency in the data). Aggregate feeder limit, not a power-flow model. The LP is a perfect-foresight relaxation used as a bound, not a deployable schedule. Not yet run: scaling to 100/250 households; feeder-limit sensitivity 8 → 6 → 5 kW/household. See PROVENANCE.md, RESULTS.md.
